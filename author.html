Sarah Johnson

Senior International Correspondent • 156 Articles

Sarah Johnson is a Senior International Correspondent based in Brussels, covering global affairs, climate policy, and international diplomacy. With over 15 years of experience in journalism, she has reported from more than 40 countries across five continents.

Sarah holds a Master's degree in International Relations from Columbia University and has received numerous awards for her investigative reporting, including the prestigious International Journalism Award and the Environmental Reporting Prize.

Before joining The News Magazine, she worked as a foreign correspondent for major international news organizations and covered pivotal events including UN climate summits, G20 meetings, and major geopolitical developments.
